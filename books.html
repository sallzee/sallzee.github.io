Warren Buffett's Recommended Books

Dive into the world of investment wisdom and life lessons with Warren Buffett's favorite reads.

The Intelligent Investor

Author: Benjamin Graham

"The best book about investing ever written." It introduces the concept of value investing, focusing on long-term strategies and thorough analysis.

Security Analysis

Authors: Benjamin Graham and David Dodd

A comprehensive guide delving into evaluating stocks and bonds, providing a foundation for value investing principles.

Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits

Author: Philip Fisher

Emphasizes investing in quality companies with significant growth potential.

The Outsiders

Author: William N. Thorndike Jr.

Insights into CEOs who excelled at capital allocation, offering valuable lessons for investors and business leaders.

Poor Charlie's Almanack

Author: Peter D. Kaufman

A compilation of speeches and talks by Charlie Munger, providing wisdom on business, investing, and life.

Business Adventures

Author: John Brooks

A collection of business stories highlighting the intricacies of corporate America.

Where Are the Customers' Yachts?

Author: Fred Schwed

"The funniest book ever written about investing," offering a humorous yet critical look at Wall Street.

The Little Book of Common Sense Investing

Author: John C. Bogle

Advocates low-cost index fund investing, aligning with Buffett's views on efficient investment strategies.

The Most Important Thing

Author: Howard Marks

Distills key investment insights, emphasizing risk assessment and market cycles.

Shoe Dog

Author: Phil Knight

A memoir by Nike's co-founder, appreciating its candid narrative on building a global brand.
